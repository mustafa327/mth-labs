Privacy & contact.

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Who is behind this website?

MTH Labs is the portfolio and studio brand of independent software developer Mustafa Thaeer, based in the Czech Republic. For website or enquiry questions, email mustafathaear97@gmail.com. Country names on the site describe remote collaboration markets, not registered offices.

The project enquiry builder

The form prepares a draft inside your browser. It does not send your entries to an MTH Labs server or form service. The site does not deliberately save your entries in cookies, browser storage or a database; your browser may offer its own autofill or restore features.

When you select Open email app or Open Gmail, the draft is passed to the application you choose. That provider handles the draft under its own terms and privacy practices. You must send the message yourself. Copy and download buttons only copy the draft to your clipboard or save it on your device.

When you email

Your message is delivered to Mustafa’s Gmail inbox, not to this website. Send only what is needed to discuss the project. Do not include account passwords, banking details, customer records or other confidential information. Contact Mustafa at the address above with questions about a message you have sent, or to request that it be corrected or removed.

Cookies, analytics and external services

This site’s code does not install analytics, advertising trackers or cookies. Fonts, illustrations, styles and scripts are built in or served with the site. The hosting provider can still process technical connection information, such as IP addresses, to serve and protect the website.

External links, including GitHub and Gmail, take you to services with their own privacy practices. This website does not control those services.

Project and payment details

This is an informational portfolio, not a checkout or a client portal. Scope, contractual terms, payments and any confidential file exchange are arranged separately. No bank details or payment processing are published here.
