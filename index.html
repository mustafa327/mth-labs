A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Before we
start.

Can you improve an existing system?+

Yes. A report, integration, workflow fix or small feature can be a sensible first project. Share the software and version you use, the current issue, and what you need to change. Please do not send passwords or live customer data.

How is a project priced?+

After reviewing the requirements, I propose a defined scope, timeline and price. For larger ideas, we can start with a smaller paid milestone. Any additional work is agreed separately.

Can we work together remotely?+

Yes. Collaboration is remote, in English or Arabic. Communication, milestones and review times are agreed around the project and our time zones.

What happens after delivery?+

The handover and any included support are defined in the proposal. Ongoing maintenance, hosting, third-party fees and future changes are discussed separately so responsibilities stay clear.
